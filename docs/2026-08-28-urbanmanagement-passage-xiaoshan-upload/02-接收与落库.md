## 现网（仅称重）

| 项 | 位置 |
|----|------|
| 客户端上云 | `IUrbanServerUploadService.SubmitRecordAsync` → `Receive` |
| 服务端 | `repos/UrbanManagement/.../UrbanWeighingRecordAppService.ReceiveAsync` |
