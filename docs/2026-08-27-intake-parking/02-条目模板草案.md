### 填写提示

- `kind`：`product` | `tech-debt` | `security` | `ops` | `docs`
- `status`：`open` | `triaged` | `absorbed` | `proposed` | `closed`
- `theme`：见 `01-设计稿.md` §3.2
- `github`：可选，如 `#42` 或完整 Issue URL
- 禁止粘贴整份接口/PRD；证据只放链接
